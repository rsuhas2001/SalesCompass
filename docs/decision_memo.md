# SalesCompass — Executive decision brief

**To:** Sales Operations Director  
**From:** Suhas Ramachandra, Portfolio Analyst  
**Scenario:** Synthetic GTM CRM, as of October 31, 2026

## Executive summary

The current simulated funnel contains **520 leads**, **122 lead-to-opportunity conversions** (23.5%), and **$4.38 million in created opportunity face value**. Among 442 contacted leads, **75.8%** received a first touch within 24 hours. An additional **78 new leads** have no contact after more than one day.

Source conversion differs materially: **Organic Search: 32.6% (28/86)**, **Inbound Demo: 28.8% (38/132)** and **Outbound: 13.8% (17/123)**. These descriptive differences could reflect acquisition mix, lead age, qualification or execution—not necessarily source effectiveness.

## Recommended operational decisions

1. **Triage the 78 overdue new leads first.** Export the lead queue, validate owners and prioritize high-value/aging records. Revisit lead routing and response SLAs.
2. **Investigate Outbound conversion before reducing budget.** Compare lead age, segment, qualification definitions, outreach recency and opportunity creation criteria.
3. **Use stage velocity to isolate workflow delays.** Review completed stage transitions by segment and campaign, while accounting for right-censoring on unfinished opportunities.

## Next measurement plan

- Track the proportion of *all* new leads reached within 24 hours, alongside contacted-only SLA and uncontacted backlog.
- Compare conversion across matched cohorts by source and lead age.
- Introduce a controlled routing or follow-up experiment before claiming incremental conversion impact.

**Limitations:** All figures are generated from synthetic data. Opportunity value is not realized revenue. No actual sales organization outcomes are implied.
